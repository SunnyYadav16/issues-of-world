Also closes the "GILGIT-BALTISTAN label inside the India outline" flag from the first slice. Relief tiles and city dots still come from OpenFreeMap / Natural Earth. |
| D-025 | `display_policy` comes from `data/sources.yaml`, not from the plugin; an unregistered source is dropped. `aliases` in states.yaml split into `search_aliases` (loose) and `geo_aliases` (strict, empty for now). Daman and Diu's old code `IN32` mapped to the merged UT | Trust the plugin's own policy; one alias list | A plugin must not be able to loosen its own license rules; "Bengal", "Punjab", "Kashmir" are fine for search but would mis-place Bay of Bengal, Pakistani Punjab and PoK stories in geoparsing. `IN32` is from FIPS 10-4 references and has **not** been seen in GDELT data: a 7-day sample (84 files, 47k rows) held `IN06` (Dadra) but no `IN32`, and also no `IN29` (Sikkim); `IN05` (Chandigarh) was confirmed. |
| D-026 | IN32 (Daman and Diu) is not mapped in `gdelt_fips`; Daman/Silvassa stories are placed by `geo_aliases` instead | Map IN32 from FIPS 10-4 references | 288 GKG files (2026-09-27 to 09-29) had IN06 30 times (Dadra and Nagar Haveli places), no IN32 and no Daman or Diu place. An unverified code could file another state's stories under DH. GDELT still uses legacy codes: none of IN40/41/42 occurred. |
| D-027 | Place search: states and India match locally (from `summary.json` names and `search_aliases`); cities and countries come from the Open-Meteo geocoding API, called from the browser. An Indian city opens its state's news with city / state / national tabs (city = headlines naming it); a place outside India flies there and says there is no news | Bundle a city gazetteer; Nominatim (its policy bars search-as-you-type); a hosted geocoder with a key | No key, CORS open, worldwide, nothing to host. Cost: the typed text (3+ characters, debounced) leaves the browser for open-meteo.com, and its terms cover non-commercial use, so check them or swap the provider before a public launch. It returns cities and countries but not states, hence the local half. News stays India-only (D-016), so foreign places have none. |

## Notes from the B6 basemap check (informal, not IOW-023 QA; superseded by D-024)

- J&K / Ladakh: the SoI outline is one dark line covering Gilgit-Baltistan and Ladakh including Aksai Chin; no second dashed border. The basemap still labels "GILGIT-BALTISTAN" (small caps) *inside* the India-drawn polygon, and labels Pakistan / China places nearby. **Carry into IOW-023:** decide whether to hide or override basemap place labels in disputed areas (plan §13, last risk row).
- Arunachal Pradesh: SoI outline encloses the whole state; basemap labels "ARUNACHAL PRADESH" and "Itanagar" as Indian. No conflict seen.

## Notes from the first end-to-end run (2026-09-29)

- 6 h of GDELT → 798 items stored, 735 cards across 27 states. Re-fetch stored 0 (URL dedupe works).
- Precision is visibly low, as D-016 predicted: of 85 Maharashtra cards, only 30 headlines name the state or an obvious local term; many are national stories (SEBI, Supreme Court, Adani) tagged to Mumbai or Delhi. Delhi is worse (17 of 100). Syndicated copies appear several times (no dedup until IOW-046). Measure it properly with the ADM1 geoparse gold set (IOW-061) before trusting any number here.
